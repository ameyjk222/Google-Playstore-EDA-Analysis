# Google Playstore EDA Analysis

**Project Overview:**

The project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on Google Playstore applications to understand app distribution, user engagement, rating, installation, category-wise performance. Using python, the dataset was analyzed to identify patterns and relationship between app attributes and user preferences, providing insights into application popularity and market trend. 

**Project Objectives:**

- Analyze app distribution across different categories and genere.
- Evaluate user rating and reviews patterns to understand app popularity and user engagement.
- Identify the most installed application and highest-performing categories.
- Explore app trend and content rating distribution.


**Tools & Techniques:**

**Python:**   
- Data analysis and manipulation

**Pandas:**
- Data cleaning, transformation, and aggregation
  
**NumPy:**
- Numerical Operations
  
**Matplotlib:**
- Data Visualization

**Seaborn:**      
- Graphical analysis




**Data Analysis & Approach:**

**Data Exploration**
- Examine dataset dimensions, column names, data types, and missing values.
- Identified numerical and categorical variables.
- Calculated the average application rating and number of unique categories.

**App Distribution Analysis**
- Analyzed the distribution of Free and Paid applications.
- Identified the most common content rating.
- Identified the top 5 applications based on installations.

**Rating and User Engagement Analysis**
- Identified the application with and above 4.0 rating.
- Compared average reviews between free and paid applications.

**Content Rating and Update Analysis**
- Identified applications updated during 2018.
- Explore application update trend pattern
- Compare content rating distribution between free and paid applications.


**Key Findings:**

- Overall the average application rating is 4.19, indicating generally positive user ratings across the dataset.
- The dataset contain 34 unique application categories, covering a wide range of user need and experience.
- Free applications are 8,421 records, compared to paid which is 724 applications, demonstrating the dominance of free apps in the dataset.
- From total 7,369 applicatins rated 4.0 or above, reflecting strong user satisfaction among rated apps.
- Game, communication are the leading categories by total installations, followed by productivity, social, and tools.
- Events, Education, and Art-and-design have some of the highest average rating among application categories.
- Free application receive more average reviews, compared to paid applications.
- 7,349 applications were last updated in 2018, indicates that substantial update activity during that year.
- Everyone is the common content rating for both free and paid applications.
